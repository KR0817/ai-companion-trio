## Changes and license scope

The trio was newly image-generated using these character references. Changes
include a shared three-character composition, new state animations, a keyboard,
an open book, a large bowl of white rice, and 16 gaze poses. Rightward gait
mirrors the approved leftward frames in the same time order. Generated pixels
were extracted, registered, cleaned at transparent edges, assembled and encoded.
